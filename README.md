## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Master of Research (Information Technology) student** in Melbourne, Australia, focused on cybersecurity, security operations, threat detection, and endpoint security.

My current research focuses on the **design and evaluation of a YARA-based endpoint malware detection framework integrated with Microsoft Sentinel for Linux environments**.

I am developing practical cybersecurity skills through academic research, hands-on labs, **TryHackMe**, **Hack The Box**, and personal security projects. My goal is to begin my professional career in a **Cyber Security Analyst, SOC Analyst, or Security Operations** role and continue developing in defensive security.
